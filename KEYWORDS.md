## Disambiguation traps (do not conflate)

- "Our Lady of Grace" also names older, unrelated devotions (e.g. the Faenza /
  Cambrai images); a hit is not automatically about the Miraculous Medal.
- "Madonna del Miracolo" is the Sant'Andrea delle Fratte image (Ratisbonne,
  1842), not the rue du Bac apparitions.
- "Green Scapular" / "Escapulário Verde" is a separate Vincentian devotion
  (Justine Bisqueyburu), often listed alongside; not this subject.
